01 / VISIONWHY JARVIS EXISTS

YOUR COMPUTER KNOWS YOUR FILES.
YOUR APPS KNOW YOUR TASKS.
JARVIS SHOULD KNOW YOU'RE WORKING.

Today, every task starts with rebuilding context: open the right app, find the right file, remember the last decision, choose a tool, explain the goal again.

Jarvis OS is being built to remove that reset. You ask once. The system understands the project, chooses the right specialist, uses the right tool, and preserves the thread for next time.
